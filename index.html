mpuploader

A personal companion tool for Insect Detect camera traps

mpuploader is a small, non-commercial tool used by its developer to upload the log files of his own Insect Detect camera traps (Raspberry Pi based insect monitoring devices) to a single folder in his own Google Drive, using rclone.

It is not a public service: there are no user accounts, no sign-up and no other users. The only Google account that ever authorizes this application is the developer's own.
